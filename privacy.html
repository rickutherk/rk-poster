Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

RK Poster ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

What data the app accesses

  • Google OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) for the channels the owner connects, with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • Basic channel information: channel ID, channel name and channel picture, from channels.list.
  • Video files, titles and descriptions that the owner chooses to upload.
  • Upload results: the ID and link of each uploaded video, and any error message.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, analytics, subscribers or any other user's information.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the channels the owner selects and to show the upload status. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored, and sharing

All data is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local database file. The app has no server of its own. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, when uploading. No data is sold or shared with any third party.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies or tracking for advertising or analytics.

Data retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens and channel details are kept until the channel is disconnected.
  • Pressing Delete next to a channel in the app permanently removes its tokens and details from the local database right away.
  • Uploaded video files can be deleted from the app's local folder at any time.
  • You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working immediately; pressing Delete in the app then removes them from the computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
