Post one clip to all my channels at once

RK Poster is a personal, internal tool used only by its owner, the creator behind Ricku the RK Gaming. It is not sold or offered to the public.

Uses YouTube API Services

RK Poster uses the official YouTube Data API v3 to upload short gaming clips to YouTube channels that the owner controls. The owner signs in with Google, chooses a channel, and grants permission to upload videos and read basic channel details (channel name and ID).

By using RK Poster you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

How it works

  • The owner connects their own YouTube channels with Google sign-in (OAuth).
  • The owner uploads a clip once, writes a title and caption, and ticks which channels to post to.
  • RK Poster uploads the clip to each selected channel with videos.insert, spaced out over time.
  • Everything runs on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to any other server.

API methods used

  • videos.insert to upload the owner's clips
  • channels.list (mine=true) to show the connected channel's name
